# 🚴‍♂️ Bike Sales Dataset – Order Quantity by Country, Age Group, and Gender

This dataset contains summarized sales data showing total order quantities broken down by **country**, **year**, **age group**, and **customer gender**. It is useful for analyzing customer demographics and sales trends across regions.

---

## 📊 Dataset Columns

- **Age_Group** – Customer age range:
  - Youth (<25)  
  - Young Adults (25–34)  
  - Adults (35–64)  
- **Customer_Gender** – Gender of the customer (if provided)  
- **Country** – Countries included (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, United Kingdom, United States)  
- **Year** – Year of the sales data (currently only 2021)  
- **Order_Quantity** – Total quantity of orders  
- **Total columns** – Includes totals by country and grand total  

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

- Shows which age groups contribute most to sales in each country  
- Young Adults (25–34) and Adults (35–64) make up the largest portion of orders  
- **United States** and **Australia** have the highest total orders  
- Useful for pivot tables and charts to compare demographics across regions  

---

## 🛠 Usage

This dataset can be used for:

- Sales trend analysis  
- Customer segmentation  
- Pivot table practice  
- Data visualization exercises  

---

## 📁 File Information

- **Filename:** `Bike_Sales_Dataset.xlsx`  
- **Format:** Excel (.xlsx)  
- **Contents:** Columns for age group, gender, country, year, and order quantities
